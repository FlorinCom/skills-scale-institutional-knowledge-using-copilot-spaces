# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, and improve work across cross-functional initiatives. The process is designed around clear ownership, iterative delivery, and transparent communication. Projects begin with initiation and planning, where the team defines the problem, stakeholders, scope, milestones, dependencies, and success measures. These activities establish a shared foundation for execution and make it easier to align engineering, product, and stakeholder expectations.

Once work is underway, OctoAcme uses a structured but iterative delivery rhythm. Product Managers define outcomes and priorities, Project Managers coordinate timing, dependencies, and communications, and Developers implement features while collaborating on design, testing, and review. Delivery is tracked through project boards and work-in-progress stages such as Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done. This keeps the team focused on small, testable increments while maintaining visibility into progress, blockers, and risks.

Communication is a key part of the model. OctoAcme relies on daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, milestone demos, and regular stakeholder updates to keep everyone informed and aligned. Risk management is handled through a simple risk register that records impact, likelihood, owner, mitigation, and status, with escalation paths from the team to PMs, Product Leads, and sponsors when issues affect delivery or business outcomes. The communication approach emphasizes a single source of truth, consistent status updates, and a clear path for triage and escalation when problems arise.

Quality and continuous improvement are built into the lifecycle. Teams use acceptance criteria and definition-of-done standards, automated validation through CI, testing, security scanning, and manual QA where appropriate. After each release, the team conducts retrospectives to capture lessons, document action items, and improve future execution. Together, these practices create a project management model that balances customer value, speed, accountability, and learning.
